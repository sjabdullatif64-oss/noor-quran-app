Expand AI Teacher to Full Quran + Progressive Word Practice

The current AI Teacher is limited to only 4 levels. Please expand the learning system so the user can eventually practice the entire Quran, not just the current four levels.

1. Full Quran Learning

- Remove the artificial limit of 4 levels.
- Add the remaining Quran lessons in the same existing Teacher structure and UI.
- The user should be able to progress lesson-by-lesson through the complete Quran.
- Do not change the existing Teacher layout, speech-recognition plugin, scoring system, or completed-lesson history unnecessarily.
- Keep the existing progression/unlocking behavior where appropriate.

2. Progressive Word Practice

Introduce a gradual reading-length progression based on completed lessons.

- Lessons 1–30: practice 1 word at a time.
- After the user completes 30 lessons, increase the practice target to 2 words at a time.
- After each additional 30 completed lessons, increase the target by 1 word:
  - 30 lessons → 2 words
  - 60 lessons → 3 words
  - 90 lessons → 4 words
  - 120 lessons → 5 words
  - 150 lessons → 6 words
  - 180 lessons → 7 words
  - 210 lessons → 8 words
  - 240 lessons → 9 words
  - 270 lessons → 10 words
- Once the user reaches 10 words, keep the practice target at 10 words for all subsequent lessons. Do not increase it beyond 10.

The progression must be based on the user's actual completed AI Teacher lessons, not simply the current lesson ID or level number.

3. Important Behavior

- Existing completed lessons must remain completed.
- Do not reset the user's existing progress.
- The system should automatically select an appropriate Quran passage according to the current word target.
- Pronunciation scoring should continue working exactly as it does now, but the expected text may contain 1–10 words depending on the user's progress.
- Keep the existing retry, feedback, pronunciation assessment, and AI Teacher behavior intact.
- Make the progression deterministic and persistent so reopening the app or changing devices does not incorrectly reset the user's learning stage.

4. UI

Update the Teacher screen so the user can see that more Quran lessons are available beyond the current four levels.

If appropriate, show the current practice progression, for example:
Practice length: 3 words

Do not redesign the Teacher screen. Keep the existing visual style and layout.
